# Scout & Steward Market Methodology

## Purpose

## Core Principle
Observe first. Advise second.

## Evidence Classes
- Sold listings
- Active listings
- Exact vs near matches
- Condition

## Observation Window
- 90-day default
- Adaptive widening when evidence is insufficient
- Historical evidence retains reduced authority

## Market Baseline
- Robust center
- Sold evidence establishes demonstrated value
- Active listings do not determine baseline

## Price Agreement
- ±25% or ±$2 representative tolerance
- ≥75% = strong agreement
- unusual observations retained and acknowledged

## Market Saturation
- comparable active listing count
- listing age
- active evidence describes competition/saturation

## Confidence
- High
- Medium
- Low
- Insufficient

## Insufficient Evidence
- no manufactured recommendation
- recommended_price_cents remains null
- Portal displays "Price Needed"
- human price required before approval

## Client Presentation
- recommendation first
- factor chips summarize findings
- chips reveal explanations/evidence on request
- statistical methodology is never required knowledge

## MVP Boundaries
- what V1 does
- explicitly deferred V2 ideas
